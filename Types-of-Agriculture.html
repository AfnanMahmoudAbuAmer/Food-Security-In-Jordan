Types of Agriculture in Jordan

Introduction to Agriculture

Agriculture in Jordan is a vital sector that contributes to the national economy and food security. Due to the desert climate in most parts of the Kingdom, agricultural activities are concentrated in areas with favorable climatic and water conditions. Agriculture in Jordan includes several main types, such as rain-fed agriculture in the northern regions and highlands, and irrigated agriculture in the Jordan Valley and desert areas, which relies on groundwater resources. Agricultural crops in Jordan are diverse, including grains, vegetables, fruits, and medicinal and aromatic plants.

Rain-fed Farming

This type of farming relies on natural rainfall, mostly practiced in northern regions and highlands. It mainly includes cereals like wheat and barley.

Irrigated Farming

Using irrigation systems, mainly in the Jordan Valley and desert areas, this type supports a wider variety of crops including fruits and vegetables.

Greenhouses and Vertical Farming

Modern methods such as greenhouses and vertical farming are growing in popularity, helping improve yield and reduce water use by controlling growing conditions.
