Vertical Farming in Jordan

Overview

Vertical farming is an innovative approach to growing food in stacked layers, often within urban environments. In Jordan, this method is gaining attention as a sustainable solution to limited agricultural space and water scarcity.

Urban Initiatives

  • iPlant: A smart farming platform that enables controlled indoor cultivation using hydroponics and automation.
  • Ivvest: A startup supporting vertical farming systems for homes and businesses, promoting local food production.

Benefits of Vertical Farming

  • Efficient use of space in cities
  • Reduced water consumption
  • Year-round crop production
  • Lower transportation costs and carbon footprint

Technology Used

Vertical farming in Jordan relies on hydroponics, LED lighting, climate control systems, and smart sensors to optimize plant growth in compact environments.

Challenges

Despite its promise, vertical farming faces challenges such as high initial costs, technical expertise, and limited public awareness. Local startups are working to overcome these barriers.

Future Outlook

With growing interest in food security and sustainability, vertical farming is expected to expand across Jordan’s urban centers, supported by innovation hubs and green tech initiatives.
